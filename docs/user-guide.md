# User Guide - LandGuard AI System

Welcome to **LandGuard AI**, the predictive land acquisition delay and risk management system.

## Key Features

1. **Dashboard Analytics**: Real-time overview of national & state projects, required vs. acquired land area, high-risk land count, pending compensation, and open alerts.
2. **Projects View**: Track corridor progress, acquisition stages, predicted completion dates, and cost overrun exposures.
3. **Land Records & Survey Search**: Search parcels by survey number, landowner name, or district with risk scoring.
4. **Interactive GIS Map**: Spatial map displaying land boundaries, risk color indicators (Low = Green, Medium = Yellow, High = Red), survey numbers, and parcel popups.
5. **Document AI & Discrepancy Analyzer**: Upload Patta / Sale Deeds / Objections to auto-detect discrepancies between revenue documents and MongoDB records.
6. **AI Delay & Cost Predictions**: View explainable risk factors, feature weights, and recommended administrative/legal actions.
7. **PDF Report Generation**: Download official PDF dossiers for any project or land parcel.
8. **AI Assistant Chatbot**: Database-aware assistant answering questions with structured `FACT`, `PREDICTION`, and `RECOMMENDATION` breakdowns.
